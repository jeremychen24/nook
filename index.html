    Lock Nook in place

    iPhone can keep you inside one app until you enter a passcode. Set it up once:

    1. Open Settings › Accessibility › Guided Access and turn it on.
    2. Under Passcode Settings, set a passcode.
    3. In Nook, triple-click the side button, then tap Start.

    To get out, triple-click again and enter the passcode.
